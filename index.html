starscapes

The complex roots of integer polynomials. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom: each view enumerates exactly the polynomials whose stars are visible in it.

  • integer quadratics
  • integer cubics (all leading coefficients; PSL₂(ℤ)-invariant)
  • monic cubics
  • quartics of height ≤ 5
  • quintics of height ≤ 3
